About

A production-first Voice AI Engineer

I'm Kamal — a Voice AI Engineer focused on one thing: shipping voice agents that actually work when real customers call. Most demos break the moment there's background noise, an accent, a CRM timeout, or an unexpected question. I build for that other 90%.

I come from a product engineering background, so I start with the business outcome — more booked calls, faster response, higher conversion — and work backwards into the architecture. Every agent I build is engineered for reliability with durable execution, monitored end-to-end with full observability, and shipped through automated CI/CD pipelines.

  • ◆ Trained under Kwindla Kramer's Voice AI Masterclass (founder of Pipecat)
  • ◆ Open-source contributor to LiveKit, PydanticAI & ACI.dev
  • ◆ Builds with handoff and documentation in mind — no vendor lock-in to me
