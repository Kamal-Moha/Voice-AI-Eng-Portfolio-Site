Questions

You're probably wondering…

When does Dalab launch?

We're opening to a small group of early users in Uganda first. Join the waitlist and we'll message you on WhatsApp as soon as your spot is ready.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Dalab works inside WhatsApp. You call it, voice-note it or chat with it just like any contact.

Is Dalab a bank or mobile money provider?

No. Dalab AI is a technology company. Dalab is the assistant you talk to; the payments themselves will be processed by licensed payment providers.

How do I know a payment is safe?

Dalab always reads the details back to you, and nothing is paid until you confirm with your PIN, entered privately in the chat. You get a receipt for every transaction.

Which languages will it speak?

English first. Luganda and Swahili are next on our list, because talking to Dalab should feel as natural as talking to a friend.

How much will it cost?

We'll share pricing before launch. Early waitlist members will hear first.
